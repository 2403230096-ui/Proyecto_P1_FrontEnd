Historia

Giro Express is hired to deliver a package to the Guardians, but they are attacked by Mavericks. Vent/Aile is separated from Girouette, ending up near the rendezvous point where Prairie and the Guardians are also under attack. To save them, Vent/Aile uses the package - the Biometal Model X - to Megamerge. After defeating the Mavericks, Vent/Aile joins the Guardians to use Model X to find Giro. Vent/Aile reunites with Giro, discovering he is the user of Model Z and secretly already a Guardian. Shortly after, a city comes under attack by Mavericks, so Vent/Aile and Giro rush to the scene. However, Giro is overpowered by Serpent, Prometheus, and Pandora. Serpent brainwashes Giro using Model W, forcing him to fight Vent/Aile. Afterwards, Serpent kills Giro and departs to let the Mavericks clean up. Desperate, Vent/Aile Double Megamerges with Model Z to create Model ZX, and escapes. Vent/Aile returns to the Guardians and plans to liberate the other four Biometals held by Serpent's forces. They hunt the Pseudoroids working for Slither Inc., in turn recruiting Models H, L, F, and P. After defeating four Pseudoroids, Serpent sends Prometheus and Pandora to assault the Guardian Base, but Vent/Aile repels them. Once all eight Pseudoroids are defeated and all four Biometals are complete, Vent/Aile uses their passwords to open the seal to reach the Model W core before Serpent. However, Pandora intervenes and allows Serpent to extract the core. Vent/Aile infiltrates Slither Inc.'s Office to stop him, but their hatred for Serpent is used as the final key to awaken the Model W core. Despite this, the six Biometals urge Vent/Aile to protect what they love, and they defeat Serpent and Model W. Serpent dies, and Model W is apparently destroyed. After the explosion, Vent/Aile is visited by the Cyber-elf of Giro, who urges them to continue fighting for peace. They then reunite with Prairie and the Guardians, promising to work for a better future.
